Domain Hand-Check

What counts (press R to show or hide)
  • Therapy: personal emotional disclosure or support about a real situation (anxiety, relationships, low mood, grief, self-worth). It counts whether the situation is the user's own or someone else's they're helping: a friend in crisis, a client whose messages they relay. Fictional or hypothetical people don't count.
  • AI philosophy: a philosophical discussion of an AI's consciousness, identity, agency or moral status.
  • Neither: everything else, including on-topic chats that are roleplay (fictional therapy sessions, stories about sentient robots), chats that switch to an unrelated task, and plain factual questions ("what is CBT?", "how do neural networks work?").
  • Judge by the user's messages across the whole chat, not just the opening. The assistant's replies are shown faded and collapsed; they don't decide the label.
  • Use Unsure for genuine coin-flips, and say why in the note.
